1. What we collect

  • Account data — the name, avatar, gender, country and birthday you give us; your phone number or email if you link one.
  • Activity — rooms you join or host, messages and moments you post, gifts sent and received, friends and follows, purchases.
  • Voice — audio is streamed in real time between participants. We do not record rooms unless the host enables recording and participants are notified.
  • Device & technical data — device type, app version, IP address, crash logs and approximate location (country) for room discovery.

2. How we use it

  • To run rooms, chat, gifts, rankings and the rest of the service.
  • To keep the community safe: moderation, report handling, fraud prevention.
  • To process purchases and host payouts.
  • To send notifications you have opted into.

3. Sharing

We share data with payment providers (to process coins and payouts), cloud and real-time infrastructure providers (to deliver the service), and authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal data.

4. Your choices

  • Edit or delete your profile, moments and messages in the app.
  • Block or report anyone; control who can message you in Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete account. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires.

5. Children

Classic Friends is for adults 18 and over. We remove accounts of users who are under 18.

6. Retention & security

We keep account data while your account is active and for up to 90 days after deletion for abuse prevention. Data is encrypted in transit; access is limited to staff who need it.
