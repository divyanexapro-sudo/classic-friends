Questions, answered

Is Classic Friends free?

Yes. Listening, talking, chatting and most features are free. Coins for gifts, VIP and store items are optional purchases.

Which languages and countries?

Rooms are tagged by language — Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Urdu, Arabic, English, Bengali, Punjabi, Marathi and Gujarati — and you can filter rooms by 21 countries across South Asia, the Gulf and the diaspora.

How do hosts earn?

Gifts you receive become diamonds. Exchange diamonds for coins inside the app, or request a payout once you reach the monthly threshold (see Terms).

Do I need to download anything?

No — the web app runs in any modern browser on Android, iPhone or desktop. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen experience. Native store apps are on the roadmap.

What are CP and families?

CP is a pair bond with your closest friend; gifts between you grow it. A family is a squad of up to 100 members with a badge, level and weekly ranking.
