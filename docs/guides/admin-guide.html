CHAPTER 1

What the console does

The admin console is the control room for Classic Friends. It shows what is happening right now and lets a small team keep the community safe, the economy healthy and the home screen fresh.

🔐
Security basicsChange the default token (classicfriends-admin) before anyone but you can reach the server. Only open the console over https://. Never paste the token into chats or screenshots. Rotate it whenever a team member leaves (see Chapter 10).
📊
Dashboard

Who is online, which rooms are live, gifts and coins today, open reports, top rooms and top gifters.

👥
Users

Find anyone by name or ID. Grant coins, set VIP, ban or unban.

🎤
Live rooms

Every open room with host, people, mics and gifts. Open one, or end it.

🚩
Reports

Everything members have reported, with context. Dismiss, warn or ban.

🎁
Gift catalog

Prices, availability and new gifts.

🖼️
Banners & Broadcast

Home-screen campaigns and app-wide announcements.

1.1Who should have access

The console is protected by one admin token. Everyone who knows the token has every power on this list, so keep the circle small. A practical split for a growing team:

RoleUsesShould not
OwnerEverything, including the server, the token, gift prices and payouts.—
OperationsDashboard, banners, broadcasts, gift catalog, coin grants for promotions.Ban users without a report trail.
ModeratorReports, users (ban/unban), live rooms (end room).Change prices, grant coins, edit banners.
SupportUsers (look up, small coin grants for refunds/compensation).Ban, end rooms, change catalog.
CHAPTER 2

Signing in and finding your way

The console lives at /admin/ on the same address as the app.

Sign-in. Paste the admin token and press Sign in. The token is remembered in this browser until you sign out.
  1. Open https://your-domain/admin/ (locally: http://localhost:3000/admin/).
  2. Enter the admin token. It is the ADMIN_TOKEN value set on the server.
  3. Tap Sign in. If the token is wrong you will see That token was not accepted.
  4. To leave, use Sign out at the bottom of the sidebar. This forgets the token on this browser.
💡
Bookmark the pagesEach page has its own address: /admin/#users, /admin/#rooms, /admin/#reports, /admin/#gifts, /admin/#banners, /admin/#broadcast.

A live picture of the community. Glance at it at the start and end of every shift.

Dashboard. Ten tiles, top rooms by gifts and top gifters. Refreshes every 10 seconds.

3.2Top rooms and top gifters

Top rooms by gifts ranks live rooms by the total coins gifted inside them since they opened; the bar is relative to the top room. Top gifters lists the five accounts with the most XP (coins ever sent) and their Charm (coins ever received). People marked bot are simulated and can be ignored for business decisions.

🤖
About botsSimulated people keep rooms lively while the community is small. They appear in counts and lists with a bot tag. Turn them off for production with BOTS=0 (Chapter 10).

4.2Actions

Grant coins. Type an amount and save. The member's balance updates instantly, even mid-room.

+ coins

Adds coins to the account. Use it for refunds, compensation after an outage, contest prizes and promotions. Enter the amount and tap Save. Keep a note of every grant (who, how much, why) in your team's log — the console does not keep one yet.

VIP

Sets the VIP level 0–7 directly. A level set here stays until you change it (it does not expire like a purchased VIP). Use 0 to remove VIP. Typical uses: partner hosts, agency leaders, making good on a failed purchase.

Ban / Unban

Confirm the dialog to ban. The member is removed from any room immediately, sees Your account was suspended, and cannot sign in again until you tap Unban. Banning is reversible and keeps the account's history, coins and friends.

⚖️
Before you banCheck the reports page for the context, note the report ID and reason, and prefer Warned for a first offence unless the behaviour is severe (threats, scams, sexual content, underage). See the moderation policy in Chapter 6.

5.1Open

Open launches the room in the app in a new tab, using whatever member account that browser is signed in with. Sign in to the app with a dedicated staff account (for example "Classic Friends Team") so hosts recognise you. Inside the room you can listen, read the chat and use the normal member tools (report, block). You do not get host powers in someone else's room — use End room from the console for that.

5.2End room

End room closes the room for everyone after a confirmation. Members see The host ended the room and return to the home screen; the room disappears from the list. Use it for rooms that break the rules (after a warning where possible), for abandoned rooms with a disconnected host, or during an incident. There is no undo, but the host can open a new room at once.

📣
Warn firstSend a broadcast (Chapter 9) or join the room with the staff account and write in chat before ending a busy room. Members remember how they were treated.
CHAPTER 6

Reports and moderation

Members report people from any seat, chat line, profile or private chat. Every report lands here with its context.

Reports. Newest first. Open reports show three actions; resolved ones show their outcome.

6.1Anatomy of a report

ColumnMeaning
WhenTime since the report was filed.
ReportedThe account being reported — the person you may act on.
ByThe member who filed it. Repeated reports from one person about many others can be a sign of a grudge.
ReasonOne of: Abusive language · Harassment · Spam or scam · Inappropriate content · Underage user · Other. Any extra details and the room ID appear underneath.
Statusopen waiting for you · resolved dismissed · warned member was warned · ban member was banned.

6.2Handling a report

  1. Read the context. Reason, details and the room ID. If the room is still live, open it (Live rooms → Open) and watch for a few minutes.
  2. Check history. Search the reported account on the Users page and look for earlier reports about them on this page.
  3. Choose an action: Dismiss when nothing is wrong or there is no evidence · Warned when you have told the member to stop (send them a message from the staff account) · Ban user for severe or repeated violations — this bans immediately after a confirmation.
  4. Write it down. Report ID, decision and reason go in the team log so the next moderator sees the pattern.

6.3Recommended policy

BehaviourFirst timeRepeat
Rude language, spam, off-topic sellingWarnBan for 7 days (unban manually), then permanent
Harassment, hate, targeted bullyingWarn and end the room if the host allowed itPermanent ban
Scams: selling coins outside the app, asking for OTPs or payment detailsPermanent ban—
Sexual content, threats, violencePermanent ban; preserve details for authorities if needed—
Underage userBan and delete personal data on request—
False or vengeful reportingDismiss; warn the reporter if it repeatsBan the reporter after repeated abuse
⏱️
Response targetsLook at open reports at least every 2 hours during your busy evening window and within 24 hours otherwise. Underage and scam reports come first.

7.1Changing a price

Price dialog. Enter the new price in coins and save.
  1. Tap Price on the gift's row.
  2. Enter the new price in coins and tap Save.
  3. The server uses the new price at once. Members see it the next time they sign in (the app loads the catalog on sign-in).

Keep the shelves readable: Hot 1–80, Lucky 10–100, Luxury 100–50,000, VIP 300–30,000. Changing tiers or VIP levels is done in shared/catalog.js.

7.2Disable, enable and add gifts

  • Disable hides a gift from the panel without deleting it; Enable brings it back. Useful for seasonal gifts.
  • + New gift asks for a name, an emoji and a price. The tier is chosen from the price (15+ coins → banner, 500+ → full screen) and the gift goes on the Hot shelf. New gifts appear for members on their next sign-in.
  • Catalog edits live in the server's memory. Make permanent changes in shared/catalog.js as well, or they will be lost on the next restart.
🎯
Pricing that worksKeep a 1-coin gift (the Rose) so everyone can say hello. Put the most beautiful effect on something many people can afford (the Crown at 500). Raise luxury prices only when the top rankings are dominated by a handful of accounts.
CHAPTER 8

Home banners

The carousel at the top of every member's home screen. Up to six banners, each with a title, subtitle, emoji and colour.

Banners. Edit the fields and the preview updates; tap Save banners to publish.
  1. Edit the Title (keep it under about 24 characters), the Subtitle (one line), the Emoji and the Colour.
  2. Use + Add banner for a new one (maximum 6) or Remove to delete one.
  3. Tap Save banners. Members see the new set the next time their home screen refreshes — usually within seconds.

Ideas that perform: weekend gift carnival with double XP, karaoke or poetry battles with a coin prize, "Family week", new-host spotlight, a festival greeting (Diwali, Eid, Onam, Pongal). Change at least one banner every week so the home screen never looks stale.

📝
Banners are promisesIf a banner announces a prize, make sure someone on the team grants it (Users → + coins) and posts the winners in a broadcast.
CHAPTER 9

Broadcast

One message to everyone: a system line in every live room's chat and a notification in every member's inbox.

Broadcast. Up to 200 characters. Tap Broadcast to send; there is no undo.

Good uses

  • Event start: "Karaoke battle starts now in Tamil pasanga singing 🎤"
  • Winners: "Congrats Harini — top singer of the week, 5,000 coins 🏆"
  • Maintenance: "We restart the server at 03:00 for 5 minutes."
  • Safety reminders: "Staff never ask for OTPs or payment details."

Etiquette

  • At most two or three per day. Broadcasts interrupt conversations.
  • Write in the languages your community speaks; keep it short.
  • Never broadcast anything about a specific report or ban.

Running the server

What the owner needs to know about the machine behind the console. Deployment to the internet is covered step by step in docs/DEPLOYMENT.md.

10.1Start, stop, restart

TaskCommand (in the project folder)
Install (first time)npm install
Startnpm start  → app at port 3000, console at /admin/
Start with settingsADMIN_TOKEN=secret BOTS=0 START_COINS=200 npm start
StopCtrl + C in the terminal (or pm2 stop classic-friends on a server)
Health checkOpen /healthz — it answers {"ok":true} with the uptime in seconds

10.2Settings (environment variables)

NameDefaultMeaning
PORT3000Network port.
ADMIN_TOKENclassicfriends-adminThe console password. Change it before going live; restart to apply.
BOTS70Simulated people. Set 0 for a real community, or a small number (10–20) while it grows.
START_COINS1000Welcome coins for every new account. 100–200 is sensible once payments are live.

10.3What is saved, and where

  • Member accounts (name, coins, diamonds, level, items, friends) are saved to server/data/users.json every 30 seconds and on shutdown, and restored on the next start. Back this file up daily.
  • Rooms, chats, moments, reports, banner edits and gift edits live in memory and are lost on restart in this version. Members simply open new rooms; banners and gift prices return to the defaults in shared/engine.js and shared/catalog.js — so make lasting changes there too.
  • For a production community, connect a database as described in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.

10.4Rotating the admin token

  1. Choose a long random token (20+ characters).
  2. Set ADMIN_TOKEN in your hosting dashboard or .env file.
  3. Restart the server. Every console session is signed out automatically.
  4. Share the new token privately with the people who still need it.
🧯
Incident checklistIf something is wrong (abuse wave, exploit, outage): 1) broadcast a short notice, 2) end affected rooms, 3) ban the accounts involved, 4) if needed stop the server, 5) write down what happened and when. Fix the cause before restarting with bots off.
CHAPTER 11

Daily, weekly and monthly routines

A small team can run Classic Friends well with these habits.

Every shift (10 minutes)

  • Dashboard: online, live rooms, on mic, coins today. Note anything unusual.
  • Reports: clear everything open. Underage and scam reports first.
  • Live rooms: skim the names and hosts; open anything that looks off.
  • Reply to support emails about coins, purchases and accounts.

Every week

  • Refresh at least one banner; plan the weekend event and its prize budget.
  • Pay out or announce event winners with a broadcast; grant prizes on the Users page.
  • Look at the top gifters and top rooms — thank the hosts, spot accounts that look like fraud (huge coin balances with no purchases).
  • Back up server/data/users.json somewhere safe.

Every month

  • Review gift prices and coin packs against what members actually buy.
  • Settle host payouts (diamonds → money) according to your Terms.
  • Rotate the admin token if anyone has left the team.
  • Read the moderation log for patterns and update the policy in Chapter 6 if needed.

CHAPTER 13

Admin API reference

Everything the console does is also available to scripts, so you can connect dashboards, alerts or a payments webhook. Every request needs the header x-admin-token: <your token>.

EndpointDoes
GET /api/admin/statsDashboard tiles, top rooms, top gifters.
GET /api/admin/users?q=&page=Search users (25 per page).
POST /api/admin/users/:idBody: { banned, vip, coins, grantCoins, level } — any subset.
GET /api/admin/roomsLive rooms with seats, members and messages.
POST /api/admin/rooms/:id/closeEnd a room.
GET /api/admin/reportsAll reports, newest first.
POST /api/admin/reports/:idBody: { action: "resolved" | "warned" | "ban" }.
GET / POST /api/admin/giftsRead the catalog / update or add a gift { id, name, emoji, price, cat, tier, active }.
GET / POST /api/admin/bannersRead / replace the banner list { banners: [...] }.
POST /api/admin/broadcastBody: { text }.
GET /healthzUptime check (no token needed).

Example — grant 500 coins from a terminal:

curl -X POST https://your-domain/api/admin/users/4821934 \
  -H "x-admin-token: YOUR_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"grantCoins":500}'

The member app talks to the server over Socket.IO with a whitelist of actions; the full event list is in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.

CHAPTER 14

Troubleshooting

When the console or the server misbehaves.

SymptomCause and fix
"That token was not accepted"The token differs from ADMIN_TOKEN on the server. Check for spaces; if the server was started without the variable, the default applies.
Status shows ● offline / "Could not reach the server"The server is stopped or unreachable. Open /healthz; if it fails, start the server (npm start or pm2 restart classic-friends) and check the hosting dashboard.
Console signs you out by itselfThe token was rotated and the server restarted. Sign in with the new token.
A member says a price or new gift is not visibleCatalog changes reach the app on sign-in. Ask them to sign out and in, or reload the page.
Banner edits disappearedThe server restarted; banners and gift edits are in memory in this version. Re-enter them, and put permanent ones in shared/engine.js / shared/catalog.js.
Members lost coins after a restartThe snapshot file server/data/users.json was deleted or the host has no persistent disk. Restore from backup; use a persistent disk or a database.
Dashboard numbers look inflatedBots are counted. Run with BOTS=0 for real figures.
Ending a room did not remove someoneTheir connection lagged. They are removed when it reconnects; a ban removes them immediately regardless.
Voice does not work for members on the live siteBrowsers allow microphones only on https://. Make sure the site has a valid certificate (see DEPLOYMENT.md).
