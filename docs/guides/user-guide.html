CHAPTER 1

Welcome to Classic Friends

Classic Friends is a live voice app. People open rooms, take a mic seat and talk — about music, cricket, poetry, late-night nothing — while everyone else listens, chats and sends gifts.

Think of it as a lounge that never closes. Every room has a host, up to twelve mic seats and a chat under the seats. You can listen for free, speak when you take a seat, and say thank you with gifts.

🎙️
Rooms

Join any public room with one tap, or open your own and take the host seat.

🎁
Gifts

Roses to Galaxies. Senders earn XP and levels; receivers earn diamonds.

🤝
Friends

Follow hosts, add friends, chat privately, pair up as CP and join a family.

What you need

  • A phone or computer with a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). No download is required — you can add the app to your home screen from the browser menu.
  • A microphone, if you want to speak. Listening and chatting work without one.
  • You must be 18 or older.

CHAPTER 2

Getting started

Signing in takes about twenty seconds. There are no forms to fill and nothing to verify before you can listen.

2.1Sign in

Sign-in screen. Pick an avatar, a name and your country.
  1. Open the app. After the splash screen you land on the sign-in page.
  2. Choose an avatar. Tap Shuffle until you find a face you like. You can change it later from your profile.
  3. Type your name. This is what people see in rooms and chats (up to 24 characters). Emoji are allowed.
  4. Pick Female or Male and your country. Your country flag appears next to your name and helps people find rooms near them.
  5. Tap Start talking. You receive a welcome gift of coins to spend on your first gifts.

The app remembers you on this device, so next time it opens straight to the home screen. Phone and Google sign-in are enabled by your app provider when the app goes live.

CHAPTER 3

Finding a room

Rooms are sorted by how lively they are. Use the tabs, topic chips, country filter and search to find your people.

Country filter. Tap the flag chip at the top of the home screen and pick a country, or All countries.
Search. Type part of a room name, a person's name, or a Classic Friends ID. Rooms and people are listed separately.
Minimized room. While you are in a room, a floating pill stays at the bottom so you can browse without leaving.

3.1Joining

  1. Tap a room card. You join as a listener — nobody hears you until you take a seat.
  2. If the room is private, type the password the host shared with you and tap Join.
  3. To leave, tap ⋮ and choose Leave room, or tap the ✕ on the minimized pill. Tapping the ⌄ arrow in the room header only minimizes — you stay inside and keep hearing the room.
🔗
Room linksEvery room has an ID (shown under its name). Friends can send you a link such as …/room/4821934; opening it takes you straight into the room after sign-in.

4.1Taking a mic and talking

  1. Tap any empty seat, or tap the mic button in the bottom bar to take the first free seat.
  2. The first time, your browser asks for permission to use the microphone. Tap Allow.
  3. You are live. When you talk, a glowing ring pulses around your avatar. The mic button turns green.
  4. Tap the mic button again to mute (it turns red). Tap once more to unmute.
  5. To leave the mic, tap your own seat and choose Leave the mic. You stay in the room as a listener.
🎧
Use earphonesEarphones stop your speaker from feeding back into your mic, so everyone hears you clearly.

Apply-to-speak rooms. Some hosts switch their room to Apply mode. Tapping a seat then sends a request; you will see Request sent to the host. You are seated as soon as the host approves.

On mic. Your avatar glows while you speak; the mic button is green when live and red when muted.

4.2Chat, emoji and reactions

Say something. Type and press Enter or the send button. Messages are limited to 300 characters and rude words are filtered.
Emoji picker. Tap the smiley in the input bar to add emoji to your message.
Reactions. Tap the smiley in the action bar for a row of quick reactions that float across everyone's screen.

Chat lines carry small badges: HOST for the host, ADMIN for room admins, a coloured Lv. badge for level and 👑 VIP for VIP members. Gift lines show in gold. System lines in purple tell you who joined and what the host announced.

4.3People in the room

User card. Tap any seat, chat line or name. From here you can send a gift, add a friend, follow, open the full profile or report.
People list. Everyone currently inside, with who is on mic and who is listening.
Top gifters. The room's contribution ranking since it opened.

4.4Follow, share, minimize, leave

More menu (⋮). Listeners see this list; hosts and admins get extra items at the top.

CHAPTER 5

Sending gifts

Gifts are how you say thank you, cheer a singer or celebrate a friend. Every coin you send becomes XP for you and diamonds for the person who receives it.

Gift panel. Receivers on top, shelves (Hot · Lucky · Luxury · VIP), gift grid, quantity and Send.
  1. Tap the pink gift button in the action bar.
  2. Choose who gets it. Tap one or more faces at the top, or ALL to gift everyone on mic. Only people on a mic seat can receive gifts in a room.
  3. Pick a shelf and a gift. Prices are shown under each gift in coins.
  4. Set a quantity — ×1, ×9, ×66, ×99, ×520 or ×999.
  5. Tap Send. Tap again quickly to build a combo (×2, ×3…).

Your coin balance is shown in the top-right of the panel. + Coins takes you to the wallet.

CHAPTER 7

Hosting your own room

Anyone can host. You get the host seat, the room tools and the right to invite friends as admins.

7.1Create a room

Create a room. Name, topic, language, seats, mic mode, theme, privacy and announcement.
  1. Tap the glowing mic button in the bottom navigation.
  2. Name your room — something people will search for, e.g. Tamil singing night 🎤.
  3. Choose a topic and the language you will mostly speak.
  4. Mic seats: 8 for a cosy room or 12 for a big one (12 seats unlock at level 15).
  5. Mic mode: Free lets anyone sit; Apply makes people request a seat and you approve them.
  6. Pick a theme. Midnight Lounge is free; other themes are bought in the Store.
  7. Turn on Private room and set a password if you want an invite-only session.
  8. Write an announcement (rules, schedule) and tap Go live.

7.2The host seat and your tools

CHAPTER 8

Moments

Moments is the app's feed — short posts from people you follow and from the whole community.

Feed. Latest shows everything; Following shows people you follow or are friends with; Hot ranks by likes and comments.
Post a moment. Tap the + button. Write up to 500 characters, optionally add a colour card and an emoji.
Comments. Tap the speech bubble under a post to read and write comments.
  • Tap the heart to like; tap again to unlike. The author gets a notification.
  • Tap a name or avatar to open that person's profile.
  • Posting a moment completes a daily task (see Chapter 11).
CHAPTER 9

Chats and notifications

Private messages, gifts in chat, and the notification centre.

Chats. Your conversations, newest first, with unread counts. The Classic Friends Team entry holds official messages; Friend requests appears when someone wants to connect.
A conversation. Your messages are on the right. If the other person is in a room, tap their name to join it.
Notifications. Gifts, follows, friend requests (accept right here), CP requests, level-ups, likes, comments and system news.

9.1Starting a chat

Open someone's card or profile and tap Message (shown once you are friends). From the chat you can send emoji and gifts — gifts in chat work like gifts in a room. The ⋮ menu lets you view their profile, block them or report them.

9.2Who can message you

By default anyone can message you. To limit it to friends only, open Me → Settings → Who can message me. Blocked people can never message you.

12.1Staying safe

Report. From any card, profile or chat choose a reason. Reports go to the moderation team with full context.
Light theme. Easier on the eyes in daylight.
Help. Short answers to common questions and a button to email support.
  • Block anyone who makes you uncomfortable — they can no longer message you, send requests or join rooms you host.
  • Report abuse, scams, harassment or underage users. Hosts and admins can act instantly inside a room; the moderation team acts across the whole app.
  • Never share your password, OTP or payment details in a room or chat. Classic Friends staff will never ask for them.
  • Coins can only be bought inside the app. Anyone selling coins elsewhere is a scam.
  • Use Private room with a password for sessions meant for friends only.
CHAPTER 13

Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for the questions we hear most often.

ProblemTry this
Nobody can hear meCheck the mic button is green (not red = muted). Make sure your browser has microphone permission for the app — look for a mic icon in the address bar. On iPhone, Safari must be the browser you are using, not a link preview.
I can't hear the roomTap the speaker button in the action bar (it may be muted). Raise your phone volume. On some phones the first sound needs one tap on the screen — tap anywhere in the room.
"Microphone blocked" messageThe browser was denied mic access. Open the browser's site settings for Classic Friends and allow the microphone, then reload.
My voice echoesSomeone (maybe you) is using a speaker near a mic. Use earphones, or mute yourself while listening.
"Not enough coins"Open Me → Recharge to buy a pack, exchange diamonds, or earn coins through check-in, tasks and lucky bags.
"Seat is locked" / "Request sent"The host locked that seat, or the room is in Apply mode — wait for the host to approve you, or ask in chat.
Connection lost / reconnectingYour internet dropped. The app reconnects by itself and puts you back in your room. If it does not, reload the page.
"Demo mode" messageYou are using the offline demo. Rooms are simulated and nothing is saved. Open the live app address to use the real service.
I forgot my accountYour account lives on the device you signed up with. Once phone or Google sign-in is enabled, link it in Settings → Phone & email so you can sign in anywhere.
